Why not just four letters?

Four letters describe your energy source, information intake, decision basis and life rhythm. But two people who are both INTJ can still differ completely in how fast they drive decisions and how visibly they express warmth.

So we add two site-specific dimensions: A/O decision momentum — whether you act once the information is good enough, or keep observing and comparing before you move; and H/C expressive warmth — whether your emotions and goodwill show openly, or live inside a steadier, more reserved style.

16 × 4 = 64 combinations bring the description closer to the everyday you. Neither new dimension has a better side: slow isn't indecisive, and reserved isn't cold.

The Six Dimensions

Energy Source

E / I

E・Outward engagement：Gains energy from external interaction and action — thinking out loud, sorting ideas while talking.

I・Inward reflection：Recharges through inner reflection and time alone — thinking things through before speaking.

Information Intake

S / N

S・Concrete sensing：Prefers concrete information, facts and hands-on experience; values what is certain right now.

N・Abstract intuition：Prefers patterns, possibilities and abstract concepts; often thinks about what things imply.

Decision Basis

T / F

T・Logical weighing：Prioritises logic, consistency and principles when deciding; cares about arguments that hold up.

F・Values and people：Prioritises values, relationships and impact on people; cares about empathy and harmony.

Life Rhythm

J / P

J・Structure and planning：Prefers structure, plans, decisions and visible progress; feels settled once things are decided.

P・Flexibility and exploring：Prefers flexibility, exploration and keeping options open; likes to figure things out along the way.

Decision Momentum (site-specific dimension)

A / O

A・Action-oriented：Action-oriented: tends to decide and move once the information is good enough, adjusting on the go.

O・Observant-reflective：Observant-reflective: tends to keep observing, weighing risks and preserving room to adjust.

* Site-specific dimension, not an official MBTI facet

Expressive Warmth (site-specific dimension)

H / C

H・Humanly expressive：Humanly expressive: emotions and goodwill are easy for others to feel — direct, warm expression.

C・Composed-reserved：Composed-reserved: expression is restrained and steady, with care shown through actions and boundaries.

* Site-specific dimension, not an official MBTI facet

FAQ

Is this the official MBTI test?＋

No. This is an independent self-exploration tool. The first four letter pairs use a common vocabulary of personality preferences; A/O and H/C are our own exploratory dimensions. We are unaffiliated with any official testing organisation.

How accurate is it?＋

We claim no accuracy figures. Percentages express the strength of this sitting's answers — not personality purity or scientific certification. Personality shifts with context and time; treat the report as a starting point.

Do I need to register or hand over personal data?＋

No. No login, and we collect no names, emails or any personal data. Your answers are stored only in your own browser and never uploaded.

Can I leave partway through?＋

Yes. Progress saves automatically after every answer — refresh or come back later and continue where you left off.

Can results be used for hiring or diagnosis?＋

No. This test provides no medical, psychological or personnel-selection conclusions. Please don't use results for any screening or diagnostic purpose.
